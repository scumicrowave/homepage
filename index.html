About

Dr. Changjun Liu received the B.S. degree in Applied Physics from Hebei University, China, in 1994, and the M.S. degree in Radio Physics (1997) and Ph.D. degree in Biomedical Engineering (2000) from Sichuan University, China. He was a post-doctoral researcher at Seoul National University, Korea (2001–2002) and a visiting scholar at Ulm University, Germany (2006–2007).

Since 1997 he has been with Sichuan University, where he became an Associate Professor in 2000, a full Professor in 2004, and a Ph.D. supervisor in 2005. He currently serves as Executive Deputy Director of the Key Laboratory of Wireless Power Transfer of the Ministry of Education, China, and as Head of the Radio Physics Division.

He has authored two books and more than 200 articles, and holds over ten patents. His group works on microwave and millimeter-wave wireless power transmission from S-band to X-band, over distances from meters to kilometers, including wireless power transmission inside closed cavities and high-efficiency microwave rectification with wide dynamic power ranges.

Research Interests

  • Microwave & millimeter-wave wireless power transmission (MWPT)
  • High-efficiency microwave rectifiers and rectennas
  • RF / electromagnetic energy harvesting
  • High-power magnetron sources and coherent power combining
  • Space solar power stations
  • Microwave heating and industrial applications
  • Antennas, frequency-selective surfaces and microwave circuits
