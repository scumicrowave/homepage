Abstract

In this papera facilitated cell model has been built to analyze the electromagnetic pulses（EMP）in- duced transmembrane potentials．The analysis shows that the athermal biological effects are caused by micropores on the membrancenot directly by transmembrane potentials．These micropores are enlarged by weak transient EMP and finally lead to membrane openings to make ions cross．The electroporation
