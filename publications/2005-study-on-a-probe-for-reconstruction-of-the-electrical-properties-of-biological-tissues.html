Abstract

A wide-band patch probe fed by coaxial line is used for reconstruction of electric properties of layered tissues．The field distribution of a patch probe in three-layer tissues is analyzed by the finite difference time domain(FDTD)meth- od，and the optimum design of the probes is performed by using genetic algorithm (GA)．As a result，a circle patch probe with symmetrical structure is designed in this paper．This probe can penetrate layered tissues deeply and concentrate in a small region and is suitable for reconstruction of electric properties of local layered tissues．The simulation results
