Abstract

A substrate integrated waveguide (SIW) filter with compact size compared to traditional SIW filters are presented in this work. A band-pass filter for general purpose is designed at 5.8 GHz with relative bandwidths of 5%. The fabricated filter is on F4B-2 substrate and about 0.5λg by 1λg, which is about 50% of a conventional SIW dual-mode band-pass filter. Simulation and measurements agree well over a frequency band of 3-10 GHz. The empirical design formulae are presented as well. 1.
