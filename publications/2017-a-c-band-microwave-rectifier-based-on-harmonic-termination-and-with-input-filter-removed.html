Abstract

This paper presents a C-band rectifier employing a third harmonic termination which is realized by a Ȝg/12 short-ended microstrip transmission line. The proposed harmonic termination balances the capacitive impedance of the diode at the fundamental frequency and blocks harmonic current of the diode. The input low-pass filter or matching circuit is removed and the bandwidth of rectifier is enhanced. Theoretical analysis and experiments are carried out. The results show that the proposed topology can realize high efficiency with a wide input frequency range. The fabricated rectifier has a compact dimension of 14mm by 22mm. A maximum RF-DC conversion efficiency of 75.6% at 13 dBm input power is observed. The measured efficiency remains above 70% with the operating frequency form 5.2 GHz to 6 GHz.

Keywords: Broadband, Schottky diode, rectifier, harmonic termination, source-pull, high efficiency
