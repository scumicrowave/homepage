Tunable Isolator-Free Magnetron Power-Combining System Based on Peer-to-Peer Locking Technique With a Three-Stub Tuner

S. Wang, X. Zhu, X. Chen, B. Yang, L. Yan, N. Shinohara, et al.

Abstract

Magnetrons are widely used microwave sources in industrial applications. To overcome the power limitations of a single magnetron and achieve large-power output, a compact, high efficiency, and straightforward magnetron power-combining system based on peer-to-peer injection locking is proposed. An H-plane tee integrated with a three-stub tuner is employed as a coupling network between two magnetrons, with the coupling characteristics adjusted via the insertion depths of the stubs. The injection-locking characteristic is qualitatively analyzed and experimentally verified. Without circulators or external injection sources, the system based on two commercial magnetrons achieves the phase synchronization through peer-to- peer injection locking. The proposed system achieves a maximum microwave efficiency of 90.1% at an output power of 1440 W. It maintains 87.8% efficiency at a peak output power of 1750 W. This work provides a novel perspective on the development of coherent magnetron power-combining systems based on peer-to- peer injection-locked magnetrons.

Keywords: High efficiency, injection locking, magnetron, peer-to-peer locking, power combining.
