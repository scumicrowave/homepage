摘要

Two novel compact substrate integrated waveguide (SIW) band-pass filters are presented in this work. They operate in dual mode at 5.8 GHz with high selectivity of a relative bandwidth of 2% and 5%, respectively. The filter has the dimension of λg×0.6λg, which is about 60% of a conventional SIW dual-mode band-pass filter. Simulation and measurements agree well. The empirical design formulae are presented as well. 1.
