摘要

A more generalized probabilistic statistical model simulating the multipath fading electromagnetic environment in re- verberation chamber has been proposed recently, which is super- position of the M plane waves with strong amplitude and the N−M plane waves with weak amplitude. But it has difficulty in obtain- ing a closed-form solution for the probability density function (PDF) of the amplitude of the rectangular component of the electric field. This paper proposes to approximate the theoretical PDF of the am- plitude of the rectangular component of the electric field with the formula of the Rician distribution under the condition of M > 5, and the approximation formula is verified by MC simulations and data fitting.

关键词: Probability Density Function, Rician Distribution, Reverberation Chamber, Monte Carlo Simulation.
