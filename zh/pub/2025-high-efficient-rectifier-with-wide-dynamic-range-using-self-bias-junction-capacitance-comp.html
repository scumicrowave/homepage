摘要

This letter presents a novel rectifier assisted by a self-bias junction capacitance compensation (JCC) for high- efficiency RF-to-dc conversion across a wide input power range. Unlike conventional tunable matching networks at the input, the proposed approach dynamically turns on the JCC in parallel with the rectifying diode by sampling the output voltage. This com- pensation mitigates intrinsic junction capacitance drift caused by the input power, significantly enhancing impedance stability without introducing extra matching circuit or requiring external bias power. The power consumption of the proposed JCC is less than 0.2% of the output dc power, making it negligible. Measured results show that the power conversion efficiency (PCE) remains above 50% over the input power range of −2 to 24 dBm, and exceeds 70% between 8 and 22 dBm, with a peak efficiency of 81.5% at 21 dBm. Compared to a reference design with a fixed impedance matching, the proposed JCC significantly broadens the ranges of input power and dc load at the same time.

关键词: Diode, junction capacitance compensation (JCC), RF rectifier, wide input power range, wireless power transmission (WPT).
