摘要

In this paper, a high-efficiency broadband rectifier is proposed, which relies on a small coupled matching structure to ensure the compactness and high efficiency of the rectifier. The proposed rectifier incorporates a series-coupled matching network and cascaded band-stop structures, with the wideband response mechanisms analyzed through theoretical modeling. Highly compact rectifiers are designed, fabricated, and measured to validate the proposed approach. The broadband rectifier, operating between 1.8 and 3.0 GHz with a relative bandwidth of 50%, achieves above 70% rectification efficiency and a peak efficiency of 79%. Moreover, the physical dimension of the rectifier is 15.8 × 18.9 mm², demonstrating its compactness. It is highly compact with high rectification efficiency, making it suitable for wireless power transmission systems and energy harvesting applications.

关键词: broadband, coupled structure, energy harvesting, rectifier, wireless power transfer.
